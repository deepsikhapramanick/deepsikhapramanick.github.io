A Little Bit About Me

Bridging the gap between ideas and reality

Hi! I'm Deepsikha Pramanick, a Data Science & AI undergraduate student interested in computational approaches to scientific and engineering problems.

My academic path has crossed mathematics, physics, engineering and data science and artificial intelligence as I find it fascinating to explore solutions to real world problems with interdisciplinary approaches.

I am currently a third-year student pursuing a B.Sc. (Hons.) in Data Science & Artificial Intelligence at the Mehta Family School of Data Science and Artificial Intelligence, IIT Guwahati.

I am currently working under the supervision of Dr. Rajiv Kumar Kar [Assistant Professor, Jyoti and Bhupat Mehta School of Health Sciences & Technology Associate Faculty, Centre for Nanotechnology Visiting Faculty, CoN, AIIMS Guwahati].

My work explores computational and data-driven approaches to problems spanning scientific computing, molecular systems, machine learning, signal processing and engineering.
